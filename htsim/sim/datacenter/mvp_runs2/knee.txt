Interpretation: REPS reroutes around degraded uplinks AND trimming + delay-based NSCC
absorb the asymmetry (excess becomes header-trims + sender pace-down), so the 1xBDP
buffer never builds a standing C_cc floor. The irreducible congestion lives in
goodput-deficit / trim-rate space, not queue-occupancy space. => queue-based C_cc is
the wrong proxy for "irreducible congestion" in a trimming network.
